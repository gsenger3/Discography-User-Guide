Welcome to Discography

Discography is a personal music collection manager for iOS and macOS. It lets you catalog physical and digital media — vinyl, CD, cassette, and more. Catalog your entire physical media collection, enrich your records with metadata sourced from the MusicBrainz database and use robust search functions to find a cataloged release in seconds.

Features

  • Universal App compatible with macOS and iOS (iPhone/iPad).
  • No subscription or login required.
  • Supports iCloud sync so your collection is synched between your Mac, iPhone and iPad.
  • Catalogs and tracks physical media collections - focused on music collections.
  • Includes a non-cluttered Home screen to display collection statistics, recently added releases and an Album Spotlight.
  • Supports popular fields and categories for organizing a large physical media collection.
  • Supports user defined tags for categorizing media beyond the native Discography fields and categories.
  • Supports album artwork.
  • Implements a robust search capability, raning from simple free text filter search to an advanced boolean search to find your music quickly.
  • Integrates music search via the MusicBrainz database.
  • Supports adding releases directly from MusicBrainz.
  • iOS Supports barcode scanning to peform lookups at MusicBrainz.
  • Implements basic and advanced MusicBrainz searching.
  • macOS Supports the ability to import collections from Discogs.
  • macOS Supports the ability to import/export Discography collections.

Download

Discography is a Universal App that is available for both macOS and iOS. Download it from the Apple App Store.
