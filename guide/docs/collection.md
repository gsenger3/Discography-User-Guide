# Managing Your Collection

This page covers the different ways to create, duplicate, and delete albums in your collection.

---

## Creating Album Entries

There are three ways to create a new album:

1. **Manually** — tap the **+** button in the toolbar (or Add New Album on the iOS Home screen). A blank edit form opens. Fill in at least the Title and Artist, then tap Save.

2. **From MusicBrainz** — search MusicBrainz, open a release detail view, then tap **Add to Collection**. The album is imported with full metadata and cover art automatically. See [Importing from MusicBrainz](musicbrainz.md#importing-from-musicbrainz).

3. **Via Barcode** — scan a UPC/EAN barcode. The app switches to MusicBrainz search mode and searches for matching releases. See [Barcode Scanner](musicbrainz.md#barcode-scanner).

---

## Duplicating an Album

Right-click (or long-press) any album row in the list and choose **Duplicate Album**. A copy opens in edit mode with "(Copy)" appended to the title. Tracks, credits, and tags are all duplicated; the copy starts with its own unique ID and no MusicBrainz link.

---

## Deleting Albums

- `iOS` **Swipe left** — on a row in the collection list to reveal a Delete button.
- **Right-click / long-press** — a row and choose **Delete Album** from the context menu.
- `macOS` **Select** — an album, then use the Delete toolbar button to remove it.
- `macOS` **Select multiple** — by clicking rows while holding Command or Shift, then use the Delete toolbar button to remove them all at once.
