# Privacy Policy

**Discography** for iOS and macOS

**Developer:** Primo Studios / George Senger

**Effective date:** October 26, 2026

## Summary

Discography does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. We have no servers that receive your information, and we use no analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services.

## Information We Collect

**None.** The developer does not collect any data from you or your device. This includes, but is not limited to:

- Personal information (name, email address, contact details)
- Usage data or analytics
- Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
- Location data
- Diagnostics or crash data
- Purchase history

Discography contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Your Collection Data

The albums, wishlist items, tracks, credits, tags, notes, and cover art you enter into Discography are stored on your device. They are not sent to the developer.

### iCloud Sync

If you are signed in to iCloud and have iCloud enabled for Discography, your collection is synchronized between your own devices using Apple's iCloud service. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account and is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The developer cannot access your iCloud data.

### Import and Export

Files you export (such as `.dsc` files or `.csv` files) are saved to a location you choose. Files you import are read only from locations you select. Neither is transmitted to the developer.

## Third-Party Services

### MusicBrainz

When you use MusicBrainz features (searching, Match Album, Fix Match, importing a release, or looking up a scanned barcode), Discography sends your search terms or the release identifiers (such as title, artist, barcode, or MusicBrainz ID) directly from your device to [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org) and retrieves release metadata and cover art in return. This communication is between your device and MusicBrainz; it does not pass through any server operated by the developer, and the developer receives no copy of it. MusicBrainz's handling of requests is described in the [MetaBrainz Privacy Policy](https://metabrainz.org/privacy).

These features are optional. Discography works for cataloging without them.

## Device Permissions

Discography may ask for the following permissions. Each is optional and used only on your device:

- **Camera (iOS):** used to scan UPC/EAN barcodes. Images are processed on your device and are not stored or transmitted.
- **Photos (iOS) / File access (macOS):** used only when you choose to select an image for album artwork, or save cover art to your Photos library. Access is limited to what you select.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
